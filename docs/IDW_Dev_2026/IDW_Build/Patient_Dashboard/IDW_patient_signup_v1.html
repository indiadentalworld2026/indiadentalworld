Founder view. Why phone-first: most Indian patients use WhatsApp and OTP daily, and a password page loses sign-ups.
  • Searchability: sign-up page is noindex; the homepage and clinic pages carry the traffic.
  • Revenue: none here. No ads, no sponsored content, no third-party scripts on this page.
