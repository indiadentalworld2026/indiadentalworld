# Manage your dental business on IndiaDentalWorld

Clinics, labs, vendors and institutes sign in the same way: your registered mobile number and a code. No password to lose.

- **Only the number on your registration**Codes go to the mobile number you registered with.
- **Check your application**Signed in before you are verified? You can see where your application stands.
- **Your data, any time**The business owner can download or delete the business data from the dashboard.

**Founder view.** One login page serves all four business types, so we maintain one flow. The homepage links (/clinic-login, /lab-login, /vendor-login, /institute-login) open this page with the type already selected.

- **Revenue:** none here. The plans and any paid features appear inside the dashboard, never on login.
- **Legal:** verified status is read from the verification list, not from this page. Unverified businesses can sign in but get no badge and no public listing.
