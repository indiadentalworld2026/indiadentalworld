WHAT MAKES THIS SEARCH BAR DIFFERENT — 10 DESIGN DECISIONS
01 INTENT ENGINE

Understands symptoms, not just keywords

"My tooth is killing me" → Emergency/Pain filter activated. "Crooked teeth" → Orthodontics. Natural language parsed before keyword matching.

02 TWO-FIELD SPLIT

What + Where, always separated

Prevents the "dentist Bengaluru" single-box ambiguity. Location autocompletes to locality level — not just city — because patients think in neighbourhoods.

03 INTENT CHIPS

Zero-typing entry for the six most common searches

Most patients arrive with one of six needs. Chips pre-fill both fields and activate the right filters — one tap, no typing.

04 RICH SUGGESTIONS

Clinic cards inside the dropdown

Each suggestion shows name + specialty + rating + distance + next available slot — enough to decide, without opening a results page.

05 AI INTENT BANNER

Shows what the system understood

"We understand you need emergency pain relief" — builds trust that the system is working for you, not just matching words.

06 VOICE SEARCH

Speak your concern, not a query

Voice input with live transcript. Especially critical for patients in acute pain who can't easily type, and for regional language speakers.

07 CONTEXTUAL FILTERS

Filters appear based on what you typed

Typing "kids dentist" surfaces a Paediatric filter. "Female doctor" surfaces the gender filter. Filters stay hidden until relevant — not 12 options always visible.

08 GPS FIRST

Use current location is always the top option

The most common patient action is "near me." GPS option is always the first suggestion in the location dropdown, never buried.

09 SLOT IN THE CARD

Next available slot shown before the patient clicks

"Today 4:30 PM" answers the patient's first question — can I be seen soon — without requiring them to open the clinic page.

10 ZERO GATING

All results visible without login

Login is required only at the moment of booking, not before. Gating the search kills both conversion and SEO indexability simultaneously.
