## FAQs

Do you accept cashless insurance?

Yes. We are empanelled with CGHS, ECHS, and major TPAs including Star Health, Niva Bupa, and HDFC ERGO. Bring your insurance card and valid photo ID.

How long does a dental implant take?

Implant placement is 45–60 minutes per implant. The final crown follows 3–4 months later after osseointegration. Digital planning with CBCT reduces complications significantly.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Covered basement parking for 12 vehicles is available at no charge for patients. Additional paid parking is available 100m away on 5th Cross.

Are your doctors NDC registered?

Yes. All treating doctors hold valid National Dental Commission (NDC) registration numbers, verifiable on the NDC portal. NDC replaced the Dental Council of India on 19 March 2026.

Do you offer EMI for expensive treatments?

Yes. No-cost EMI is available for treatments above ₹15,000 via HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Bajaj Finserv. Discuss with our front desk at the time of booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Appointments cancelled with more than 4 hours' notice incur no charge. Later cancellations may be subject to a ₹200 scheduling fee. Emergency rescheduling is always accommodated.

How is patient data protected?

We are fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2025 (DPDP). Before/after photographs and treatment records are stored with explicit patient consent and never shared without permission.

Are children's appointments available on weekends?

Yes. Our paediatric dentist visits every Thursday, Friday, and by appointment on Saturdays. Sunday slots for children are available 10 AM – 12 PM.
