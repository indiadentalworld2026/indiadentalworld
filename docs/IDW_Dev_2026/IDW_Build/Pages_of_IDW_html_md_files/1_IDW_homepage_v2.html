Questions people ask.

How does IDW verify a clinic?

We check the clinic's DCI registration and confirm its address before the Verified badge appears. Clinics still being checked show "Verification pending".

Is IDW free for patients?

Yes. Searching and comparing clinics is free and needs no sign-in. Clinics pay IDW, not patients.

Are the prices final?

No. Prices are indicative ranges shared by clinics. The final cost depends on your examination and treatment plan, and the clinic confirms it before treatment.

How do you rank clinics?

By distance, verification, profile completeness, rating, current availability and, to a small degree, the clinic's plan. Up to two listings on a results page can be paid placements. They are always labelled Sponsored and never change the order of the rest.

How do reviews work?

Only patients with a completed appointment can review. Clinics can reply in public but cannot edit, hide or buy reviews.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital. IDW does not give medical advice. For a toothache that can wait, search "Toothache" to see clinics open now.
