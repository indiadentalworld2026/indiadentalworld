🏛️ CGHS / ECHS Empanelment Details
CGHS dental procedures covered: extraction, scaling, RCT, dentures, crowns. Implants not covered under CGHS. ECHS covers basic restorative. Reimbursement at CGHS rates — do not collect additional fee from the beneficiary.
🇮🇳 Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) Empanelment
PM-JAY covers select dental procedures under HBP (Health Benefit Package). Verify applicable dental HBP codes with your state's nodal agency before empanelment. Do not advertise PM-JAY coverage for procedures not covered under your approved HBP codes.
