The 3-Layer Revenue Model

Layer 1 — Transaction
Booking Commission
You earn when the patient books. Aligns your success with clinic success. This is the foundation. Build this first — everything else amplifies it.
Layer 2 — Subscription
Clinic Premium · Brand Pages
Predictable monthly recurring revenue. This is what makes you fundable. Investors look for this before writing a cheque.
Layer 3 — Advertising
Sponsored Listings · Partnerships
High margin, scales with traffic. This is what makes you a media business at scale. Valuable only once Layer 1 traffic proves it.
⚠ Founder Warning: Most platforms rush to Layer 3 before Layer 1 is solid. The result is a cluttered, untrusted platform that loses on SEO and user retention. Build Layer 1 first. Layer 3 becomes enormously valuable once you have the traffic to prove it.

Non-Negotiable Rules

Google & SEO Compliance
  • Sponsored content always labelled — "Ad" or "Sponsored" — no exceptions
  • Reviews never incentivised — Google delists platforms that pay for reviews
  • Clinic data always accurate — wrong hours = local SEO penalty
  • Page speed always protected — no ad network adding 200ms+ load time
  • Core Web Vitals must stay green even with ads loaded
LLM & AI Search Compliance
  • Every page needs clear authorship — who wrote it, their qualification (E-E-A-T)
  • Structured data makes IDW citable by AI — schema.org/Dentist on every clinic page
  • FAQ schema on every clinic page = featured snippet and AI answer eligibility
  • Keep content factual, specific, sourced — AI prefers platforms with verifiable data
GoI & DPDP Compliance
  • Never sell individual search data — DPDP 2025 violation
  • Opt-in only for all communications — no cold contact without consent
  • Affiliate commissions disclosed in T&Cs and clinic contracts
  • GST 18% on commissions — included in clinic contract from Day 1
User Experience Rules
  • No interstitial ads — pop-ups blocking content = instant Google penalty
  • No auto-play video ads — banned by Google Ad policy
  • Max 1 sponsored result per 5 organic results on search pages
  • Zero ads in booking flow — from "select slot" to "confirmed" is sacred
  • Mobile ad density — max 30% of screen at any time
